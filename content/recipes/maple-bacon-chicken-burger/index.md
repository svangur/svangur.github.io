## Instructions


- Marinade:
	- 3 tbsp mayo (45gr)
	- 1 tbsp soy (12gr.)
	- 1 tbsp syrop (12gr.)
	- Pepper to taste
	
- Burger sauce (A bit runny)
	- 60gr. sour cream
	- 60gr. mayo
	- 1.5 tbsp (18gr.)
	- 1.5 tbsp (18gr)
- 
- Bacon 3 pieces per buger
- paint with maple syrop
- Over 200c 12-14min
