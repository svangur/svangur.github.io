## Instructions

1. Break up the cold rice with your fingers so there are no clumps.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon of the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Pour in the eggs, scramble until just set, and tip out onto a plate.
3. Add the remaining oil. Stir-fry the spring onion whites and garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the rice and spread it out. Let it sit undisturbed for 30 seconds to crisp, then toss. Repeat twice more.
5. Add the chicken and peas and toss until heated through, about 2 minutes.
6. Return the eggs, add the soy sauce and sesame oil, and toss to coat. Finish with the spring onion greens.

## Tips

- Fresh rice turns gummy — if it's all you have, spread it on a tray and refrigerate it uncovered for 30 minutes first.
- Everything happens fast at wok temperature: have all ingredients prepped and within reach before you turn on the heat.
