## Instructions

1) Boil potatoes for 15-20 mins
2) Coat potatoes with olive oil (1-2 tbsp) and toss with salt + pepper
3) Placepotatoes on oven tray and bake at 200c for +20mins
4) Shallow fry the fish in 2 batches (2 tbsp oil + 1 butter)
	1) ~ 3 min per side (med high 5-6)
5) Serve with kokteilsósa and lemon.
