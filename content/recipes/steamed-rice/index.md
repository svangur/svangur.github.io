## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice in a sieve under cold water until the water runs mostly clear — this washes off surface starch that would make it gluey.
2. Combine the rice, water, and salt in a pot with a tight lid. Bring to a boil uncovered.
3. As soon as it boils, cover, drop the heat to the lowest setting, and cook for 12 minutes. No peeking.
4. Off the heat, let it steam covered for another 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

## Tips

- For fried rice, spread the cooked rice on a tray, cool completely, and refrigerate uncovered overnight — dry, separate grains fry instead of steaming.
- Scale by ratio: 1 part rice to 1.5 parts water by volume works for most white rice.
