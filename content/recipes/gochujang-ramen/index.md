## Instructions


**

Gochujang Ramen with Soy-Marinated Eggs

**

_Serves 3 | Prep time: 10 mins (+ egg marinating) | Cook time: 20 mins_

---

**1. The Ramen Eggs (Ajitsuke Tamago)**

_Prepare these at least 2 hours ahead (or up to 24 hours) for the best flavor._

- **3 large eggs** (straight from the fridge)
- **60ml** Soy sauce
- **60ml** Mirin (or water + 1 tsp sugar)
- **1** Garlic clove, smashed

1. **Boil:** Bring a pot of water to a boil. Lower the eggs in gently.
2. **Timer:** Simmer for exactly **6 minutes and 30 seconds** for a jammy yolk.
3. **Shock:** Immediately move eggs to an ice-water bath for 5 minutes.
4. **Marinate:** Peel the eggs and place them in a small jar or bag with the soy sauce, mirin, and garlic. Keep in the fridge until serving.

---

**2. The Broth & Noodles**

- **1.2 Liters** Chicken or Vegetable stock
- **300g–350g** Ramen noodles (fresh or dry)
- **25ml** Sesame oil
- **5** Garlic cloves, minced
- **15g** Fresh ginger, grated
- **80g** Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- **25ml** Soy sauce
- **8ml** Rice vinegar
- **8g** Sugar or honey

1. **Aromatics:** In a large pot over medium heat, sauté **garlic** and **ginger** in **sesame oil** for 1 minute until fragrant.
2. **Toast the Paste:** Add the **gochujang**. Stir constantly for 2 minutes until it darkens slightly and smells toasted.
3. **Simmer:** Whisk in the **stock**, **soy sauce**, **sugar**, and **vinegar**. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
4. **Cook Noodles:** While the broth simmers, boil a separate pot of water. Cook the **ramen noodles** according to the package (minus 1 minute for _al dente_). Drain well.

---

**3. Assembly**

1. Divide the cooked noodles into 3 deep bowls.
2. Ladle **400ml of hot broth** over each bowl.
3. Slice the **marinated eggs** in half and place one (two halves) on top of each bowl.
4. **Optional Toppings:** Sliced spring onions, sesame seeds, and a handful of fresh spinach or bean sprouts.
