  - 200 g spaghetti
  - 5 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  - 80 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  - 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes
  - Small bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  - Salt
  - Grated Parmesan, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook gently, stirring, until pale gold — about 3 minutes. Do not let it brown or it turns bitter.
3. Add the red pepper flakes and cook 30 seconds more.
4. Scoop out a cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti and add it to the pan.
5. Toss over medium heat, adding splashes of pasta water, until the oil emulsifies into a glossy sauce that clings to the noodles — about 1 minute.
6. Off the heat, toss through the parsley. Season, and serve with Parmesan if you're feeling heretical.

## Tips

- The garlic goes from golden to burnt fast — when in doubt, pull the pan off the heat early.
- Starchy pasta water is the whole trick here; don't skip it.
